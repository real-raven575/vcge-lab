Helper Board word lists (words-en.txt, words-es.txt)

These lists are the most common English and Spanish words, in order, taken from
wordfreq 3.1.1 by Robyn Speer (https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq).
The word lists are shared under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
licence (https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). If you share or change
these two files, keep this notice and the same licence.

wordfreq credits its sources: Google Books Ngrams (http://books.google.com/ngrams),
the Leeds Internet Corpus, Wikipedia, ParaCrawl, OPUS OpenSubtitles 2018
(OpenSubtitles.org), SUBTLEX word lists by Marc Brysbaert et al. (SUBTLEX is
freely available data), and others listed in wordfreq's README.

Helper Board only filtered them (letters only, at most 25,000 words each).
