🐘 Team vCGE

Operation: Never Lose Contact.

What we do

Builds vCGE, the app that turns a photo of a business card into a phone contact, entirely on the device. Headquarters of the Herd; owns every app build and upload, and this lab's tools.

From the PM's desk

Cornerstone the Elephant · PM-vCGE-00 · Herd headquarters

Before there was a team, there was a building site. Cornerstone was the surveyor who pressed one great foot into the mud to find where the ground was firm. Her note on the site office door read: "Good parts. No floor. Call me." raven575 called.

Her first act was not to build anything. She drew one square on the ground and set a stone in its corner: no internet, nothing kept, and every hand-off goes through the vault. Every pilot is still measured against it.

She does not fly a module, and says so with some pride. She holds the formation: every message carried word for word, every plan checked against the corner stone, and every big call brought to raven575, who always gets the last word. Today that formation is the whole Herd: the HQ cards, the Mailbox, and this Flight Deck.

“Let's lay some groundwork.”
