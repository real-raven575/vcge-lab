Glyph's handwriting bench · MOD04 OCR · English first, print and cursive

Handwriting. Everyone's is different and mine is all feathers. Run the samples and tell me what they really say.

Reads every handwriting sample with one published build's own reader, in this browser. You type (or confirm) what is really written, and each sample is scored against it: characters right, characters right ignoring capitals, and whole words right, split by print or cursive and by UPPER, lower or Mixed case.
